We present <strong>JPC</strong>, a jerk-bounded position controller that lets industrial robots track complex trajectories while enforcing dynamic constraints at the motion level, without access to servo-level control. JPC also supports real-time task modification: users can replace the ongoing task without violating dynamic constraints.
