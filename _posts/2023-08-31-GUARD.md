We introduce <strong>GUARD</strong>, a Generalized Unified SAfe Reinforcement Learning Development Benchmark. GUARD covers a wide variety of RL agents, tasks, and safety constraints, includes self-contained implementations of state-of-the-art safe RL algorithms, and is highly customizable in both tasks and algorithms.
