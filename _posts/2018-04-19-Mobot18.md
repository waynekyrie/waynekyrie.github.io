This was a project for the [CMU Mobot competition](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/mobot/race.html) in 2018. This annual competition, held by the School of Computer Science, challenges teams to build a mobile robot that follows a track in front of Wean Hall. Small gates placed along the track serve as checkpoints, and the robot must pass through each of them, so it has to follow the line strictly rather than head directly to the goal.
