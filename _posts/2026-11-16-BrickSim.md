We present <strong>BrickSim</strong>, the first real-time physics-based simulator for interlocking brick assemblies, modeling assembly, disassembly, and structural collapse with high fidelity. It integrates with a wide range of robot platforms, lowering the barrier to studying dexterous, contact-rich, long-horizon manipulation.
