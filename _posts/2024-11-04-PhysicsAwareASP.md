We formulate combinatorial assembly sequence planning as a reinforcement learning problem. An optimization-based, physics-aware action mask tackles the sim-to-real gap and combinatorial complexity, guiding policy learning and ensuring violation-free deployment by planning physically executable assembly sequences.
