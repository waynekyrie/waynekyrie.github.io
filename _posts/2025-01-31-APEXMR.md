We propose <strong>APEX-MR</strong>, an asynchronous planning and execution framework that safely and efficiently coordinates multiple robots for dexterous, long-horizon cooperative tasks. Deployed on a bimanual system, it is the first robotic system to perform customized LEGO assembly with commercial LEGO bricks.
