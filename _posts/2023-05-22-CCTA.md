We present an integrated framework for proactive human-robot collaboration. A robust intention prediction module, trained with prior task knowledge and human-in-the-loop data, guides the robot, while robust safe control ensures safety under uncertainty. We demonstrate the framework on a co-assembly task with a Kinova Gen3 robot.
