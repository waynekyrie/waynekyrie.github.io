This was a research project I did during my internship in the AirLab led by [Prof. Sebastian Scherer](https://www.ri.cmu.edu/ri-faculty/sebastian-scherer/). The goal was to enable building inspection from 3D reconstruction. We added thermal texture to the reconstruction to make inspection more reliable.
