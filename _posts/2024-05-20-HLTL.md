We propose a decomposition-based method for multi-robot planning under hierarchical temporal logic specifications. Each specification is broken into temporally related sub-tasks, relations among sub-tasks across the hierarchy are mined, and a mixed-integer linear program generates a spatio-temporal plan for each robot.
