We introduce <strong>JSSA</strong>, a jerk-based safe set algorithm that ensures collision avoidance while respecting robot dynamic constraints. It extends the original safe set algorithm, previously limited to second-order systems with unbounded acceleration, and is validated on human-robot interaction tasks with a FANUC LR Mate 200iD/7L.
