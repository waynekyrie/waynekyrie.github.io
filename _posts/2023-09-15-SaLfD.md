We present <strong>SaLfD</strong>, a simulation-aided learning-from-demonstration framework that makes it easy to deploy LEGO prototyping on robots. The robot watches a user build a custom object, extracts a construction plan, and verifies both the plan and the resulting prototype in simulation.
